WHO WE ARE

Professional advice. Personal approach.

Karanja Law & Co. Advocates is a Nairobi legal practice focused on helping clients navigate the legal dimensions of business, investment, property and personal affairs.

The firm works across banking and finance, corporate and commercial matters, legal audit and compliance, intellectual property, employment, real estate and private-client planning.

Led by Managing Partner Faith Wambui Karanja, the practice aims to give clients accessible guidance rooted in careful analysis, clear communication and attention to each matter’s specific circumstances.
