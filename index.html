About Karanja Law
& Co. Advocates

Practical counsel.

Karanja Law & Co. Advocates is a Nairobi-based legal practice serving individuals, businesses, financial institutions and investors across a broad range of legal needs.

Led by Managing Partner Faith Wambui Karanja, the firm brings a practical approach to banking and finance, commercial matters, real estate, intellectual property, compliance and private-client advisory.
