GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION · BANKING & FINANCE

Before Signing a Financing Agreement

Financing documents can set out important obligations involving repayment, interest, events of default, guarantees and security. Before signing, understand which parties are responsible, how amounts are calculated and what happens if circumstances change.

Consider obtaining legal advice on the specific agreement and the security package before execution.

GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION · LEGAL COMPLIANCE

Why Businesses Consider Legal Audits

A legal audit is a structured look at matters such as company records, licences, contracts, employee documentation, intellectual property and compliance processes. It can help decision-makers identify gaps and prioritise follow-up action.

The scope of an audit should be defined around the business, sector and applicable legal requirements.

GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION · REAL ESTATE

Questions to Ask Before a Property Purchase

Property transactions often involve title checks, identity and authority of the parties, encumbrances, consents, survey information, taxes and completion documents. The appropriate checks depend on the nature of the land and the proposed transaction.

Ask your advocate to advise on the searches and documentation needed for your proposed purchase.

GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION · DATA PRIVACY

Data Protection in Everyday Business

Businesses handling personal information should understand why the data is collected, the relevant lawful basis, access controls, retention practices and how data-subject requests will be handled.

Good governance begins with knowing what information is collected and why.

GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION · INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Protecting Your Business Identity

A brand, creative work or commercial innovation may be a valuable business asset. Early advice on registrations, contracts, ownership and licensing can help clarify the options available for protection.

Different categories of intellectual property have distinct rules and requirements.

GENERAL LEGAL INFORMATION · PRIVATE CLIENT

The Value of an Estate Plan

A considered estate plan can make it easier for families to understand intentions, assets and responsibilities. Wills, trusts and other arrangements should be considered in light of the individual’s circumstances and applicable succession rules.

Review your wishes and obtain advice before executing estate-planning documents.
